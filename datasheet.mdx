## The problem

Support teams lose the first hour of every ticket to sorting. Someone reads it, guesses the topic, guesses how urgent it is and moves it to a queue. Urgent tickets wait behind routine ones, and agents start every reply from a blank page.

## What Brightweir Triage does

Brightweir Triage reads each new ticket the moment it arrives. It sends the ticket to the right team at the right priority and drafts a reply your agent can approve in one click. When it is unsure, it says so and hands the ticket to a person.

## Built for trust

- **People decide what customers see**<br />
  The API has no way to send a reply on its own.
- **Stable results**<br />
  You choose the model version and switch only after testing a new one.
- **Your data stays yours**<br />
  Ticket text is encrypted, deleted after 30 days and processed in the region you choose (Canada, the US or the EU).
- **Clear audit trail**<br />
  Every result records the model version and confidence score.

## Fits the way you work

- **Connects to your help desk** through a REST API, with ready-made examples in curl and Python.
- **Uses your categories**, not ours, so reports stay consistent.
- **Free sandbox** for testing with synthetic models before you go live.
